# Odisha Travel & Tourism Guide

## Getting to Odisha

### By Air
Biju Patnaik International Airport (BBI) in Bhubaneswar is the only major air gateway. 
- **Terminal 1**: Domestic flights
- **Terminal 2**: International flights
IndiGo, Air India and Air India Express connect Bhubaneswar with Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Kolkata and more. Odisha also has three other domestic airports at Rourkela, Jharsuguda, and Angul, which are useful if you're headed to western Odisha rather than the coast.

### By Rail
The main railway stations are Bhubaneswar, Cuttack, and Puri, with frequent train services connecting Odisha to the rest of the country. Major long-distance trains on these routes include the Konark Express, Rajdhani, and Coromandel Express. Since roads from the north/west don't connect directly, rail is often the most convenient way in for a lot of travelers.

### By Road/Bus
Bhubaneswar's Baramunda ISBT is the state's biggest bus terminal, connecting to most major districts. 
- **Berhampur** handles the south (Cuttack, Bhubaneswar, Koraput, Rayagada, and into Andhra Pradesh).
- **Sambalpur** covers the west (Bargarh, Jharsuguda, Cuttack, Rourkela). 

State transport (OSRTC) also runs interstate. OSRTC has been modernizing fast in 2026, adding GPS-tracked buses, digital booking, and upgrading terminals under the "Atal Bus Stand" scheme. Tickets can be booked easily online through the OSRTC portal or aggregators like redBus.

## Getting Around
OSRTC runs frequent buses across Odisha's **"Golden Triangle" tourist circuit (Bhubaneswar–Puri–Konark)**, which is the easiest way to cover the classic route without a private car.

## Top Places to Visit

### Bhubaneswar
- **Description**: Odisha's capital, known as the Temple City with over 600 ancient shrines. Good base for exploring the rest of the Golden Triangle.
- **Highlights**: Lingaraj Temple, Mukteshwar Temple, Rajarani Temple, and the Udayagiri & Khandagiri caves.
- **Best Time**: October to March

### Puri
- **Description**: Coastal pilgrimage town centered on the Jagannath Temple, one of the Char Dham sites and famous for the Rath Yatra festival. Also has a long beach, local markets, and easy access to Chilika Lake and Konark.
- **Access**: Has its own railway station.

### Konark
- **Description**: Home to the UNESCO World Heritage Sun Temple, a 13th-century chariot-shaped monument covered in intricate carvings. 
- **Location**: About 65 km from Bhubaneswar, usually combined with Puri in one trip.

### Chilika Lake
- **Description**: Asia's largest brackish water lagoon, known for migratory birds, dolphin-spotting boat trips, and quiet island villages. A favourite nature stop on the Bhubaneswar-Puri route.

### Cuttack
- **Description**: Odisha's cultural capital, known for centuries-old silver filigree (Tarakasi) work, Barabati Fort, and a lively festival calendar.

### Similipal National Park
- **Description**: A large forest reserve (2,750 sq. km) known for Royal Bengal Tigers, guided wildlife safaris, trekking trails, and the Baharepani and Joranda waterfalls. 
- **Tip**: Book safaris ahead of time.

### Sambalpur
- **Description**: Western Odisha's hub, home to the Hirakud Dam (one of the world's longest dams) and famous for handwoven Sambalpuri sarees.

### Gopalpur
- **Description**: A quiet former colonial-era sea port town on the southern coastline, about 15 km from Berhampur — a low-key beach stop away from the busier circuit.

## Recommended Itinerary Notes
The classic first-timer route is the **Golden Triangle — Bhubaneswar → Puri → Konark** — all connected by frequent OSRTC buses and doable in 4–5 days. 
- **Best time to visit**: October to March, when the weather is pleasant for sightseeing.
- **Avoid**: April–June (hot) and July–September (heavy monsoon).
